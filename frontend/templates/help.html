How can we help you?

How to Report an Issue

1

Navigate to Report Page

Click "Report an Issue" on the homepage or use the navigation menu.

2

Login / Sign Up

Authenticate with your CivicConnect account to submit reports and track progress.

3

Fill in Details

Add a clear description, upload up to 2 photos, and the system will auto-detect issue type.

4

Pin Location

Use the interactive map or “Use My Location” to mark exact spot — accuracy speeds up resolution.

5

Submit & Track

After submission, you'll receive a unique Complaint ID. Track status via “My Complaints” dashboard.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can use CivicConnect?

Both citizens and municipal officers. Citizens report issues and track complaints; officers use the dedicated portal to manage and resolve cases efficiently.

How many photos can I upload?

Currently you can upload up to 2 photos per report. Clear images help AI classification and speed up department assignment.

How are reports routed to departments?

Our AI analyzes description and image to detect the issue type and automatically assigns the report to the correct department (Sanitation, Roads, Water, etc.).

How do I track my report progress?

Go to “My Complaints” in your dashboard. You can view real-time status, assigned department, and updates from officers.

What if I forgot my password?

Click “Forgot password?” on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your registered email.

How long does resolution take?

Resolution time depends on issue type and severity. Minor issues may take 2–4 days, while major infrastructure repairs can take up to 2 weeks. Live updates are available in dashboard.
